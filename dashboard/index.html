How the radar scores

Implied value = TVL × category multiple × growth factor × tenure factor × chain factor × plausibility. Every factor comes from the snapshot's own figures: the protocol's TVL, its seven-day growth, how long it has run, how many chains it runs on, and whether a token launch is plausible for it at all.

  • Category multiple — the FDV/TVL ratio the category typically commands: exchanges 0.10, bridges and wrappers 0.12–0.16, lending and derivatives 0.34–0.38, DEXes 0.42, chains 0.45.
  • Growth factor — 1 + the seven-day TVL change, clamped to −40%…+120%, over 250.
  • Tenure factor — 0.75 for a brand-new protocol, rising to 1.00 at five years; unknown age scores 0.85.
  • Chain factor — 1.00 on one chain, +0.04 per extra chain, up to 1.20.
  • Plausibility — 1.00 normally; 0.10 for an exchange, which is a company, not a protocol launch; 0.25 for a bridge or a wrapped or staked product of a token that already trades; 0.50 for a staking pool; 0.40 when the name says wrapped, staked or staking, or starts with an exchange's name.

The implied value is this radar's own claim, never make.fun's. It does not predict whether or when a token is issued. A protocol DefiLlama records no token for may still have one its record does not carry; the board says a protocol records no token, never that it has none. Farm plans are a starting point, not advice: check the protocol yourself before you deposit anything.
